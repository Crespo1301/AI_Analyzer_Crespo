# Product

## Register

product

## Users

NFL readers comparing this week's matchups and AI predictions on phones and
desktop browsers, plus Carlos preparing weekly CSolutions project updates.

## Product Purpose

Build a CSolutions prediction-comparison publication owned by Carlos Crespo.
NFL is the first category. The future brand and domain must also accommodate
predictions outside sports. Additional categories remain planned until they
have a defined source, evaluation method, and published records.

Make the current season easy to follow through schedules, preserved predictions,
auditable results, and separate reasoning reviews. Historical results belong to
their own season. This is an independent public project, not an official league
service or an automated live-score feed.

## Brand Personality

Direct, sporting, accountable. Follow the existing sports-editorial direction in
Docs/2026/redesign-direction.md and the user's supplied NBA reference boards.

## Anti-references

Generic AI dashboards, gradients, oversized empty panels, fabricated activity,
and historical results presented as current-season performance.

## Design Principles

- Lead with matchups and useful evidence.
- Use team identity to help recognition, with names alongside logos.
- Keep predictions, settled results, and missing information distinguishable.
- Preserve the static publishing workflow and the original response archive.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard navigation, readable contrast, reduced motion, and touch input.
Keep image dimensions stable and provide text fallbacks. Color is supplementary.
