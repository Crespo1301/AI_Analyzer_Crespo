About

An independent study, kept honest in public.

The AI Analyzer NFL study is a personal research and portfolio project by Carlos Crespo. It started as a senior-year experiment at the University of San Francisco in the 2025 season, then grew into a multi-year record of how large language models handle real NFL prediction under a fixed protocol.

Author
Carlos Crespo. Independent researcher, software engineer, University of San Francisco alumnus. Runs CSolutions on the side.
Affiliations
None. No affiliation with the National Football League, ESPN, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or any sportsbook. Team abbreviations and colors are used editorially to identify teams, not to imply endorsement.
Independent status
Not sponsored, not paid to publish picks, not an official tout. This site does not sell picks or premium tiers.
Not financial advice
Nothing on this site is a recommendation to bet real money. The picks are a study of model reasoning under a fixed budget. Do your own homework before staking anything.
Data policy
The site collects no personal data, sets no tracking cookies, and runs no analytics right now. If that changes it will be disclosed here.
Content policy
AI-generated content on this site is labeled by model and prompt version. Corrections are preserved next to originals so the record is auditable.
Imagery
Editorial illustrations are original brand work. Team logos, when shown, are hotlinked from ESPN's public CDN to identify teams. No fabricated player photography is used anywhere on the site.
Contact
Reach out via GitHub issues or the CSolutions site.
Source code
github.com/Crespo1301/AI_Analyzer_Crespo. Everything is open source and auditable.
License
Source code released under MIT. Data snapshots and editorial text kept for study use.

Public disclosure. This site publishes AI-generated betting research for the purpose of study and portfolio credibility. Picks are locked before kickoff and graded against the ESPN box score. Reasoning grades are separate from pick outcomes. The site is not a tout service, not a sportsbook, and not affiliated with any of them. If you rely on this site to make real-money decisions, you assume the risk.
