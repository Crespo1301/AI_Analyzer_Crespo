AI Model Performance Analyzer

Can you trust an LLM with your bankroll?

A structured, ongoing test of whether ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini can make better betting decisions than the market. Same prompt, blind chats, $20 bankroll per game, graded against the ESPN box score. Started as a class experiment in 2025. Now a live weekly season.

How this works

Simple rules, strict enforcement, full audit trail.

01

Same prompt to all three models

Identical text sent to ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini in fresh chats with no prior context. Removes prompt-style variance from the comparison.

02

$20 bankroll per game

Each model decides how to split the bankroll across straight bets, parlays, SGPs, or reserve. Sizing itself becomes a graded signal.

03

Locked before kickoff

Every pick is timestamped and pushed to this repo pre-game. No post-hoc editing. Raw model responses are saved verbatim.

04

Graded against ESPN

Final grading uses the ESPN box score, not memory or headlines. Every prop must be auditable against a real stat line.

Meet the models

Three frontier LLMs, one budget each, one blind read per game.

ChatGPT
GPT 5.5
Capital preservation. Runs break-even math before every bet. Refuses to bet without a demonstrated edge. Sizes to the edge, not to the conviction.
Claude
Opus 4.7
Thesis-driven. Builds a coherent game script, commits to correlated positions on it. Takes the underdog and the Under when the read supports both.
Gemini
3.1
Confident concentrator. Sizes big on high-conviction reads. Pays juice on favorites, stacks correlated legs into same-game parlays.

These are early archetypes based on the first two 2026 games. See the 2026 season page for evolving evidence.
